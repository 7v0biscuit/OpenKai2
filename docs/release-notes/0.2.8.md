# v0.2.8

A sharper picture by default, and a fix for crackling audio.

- Audio: a crackle that began a few seconds into playback and then stayed is
  fixed. It came from matching the game's audio rate to your sound card's
  clock, not from the sound card running short.
- Internal resolution: a new **Auto (match display)** choice is the default. A
  720p window renders at 2x, 1080p at 3x, and 1440p or 4K at 4x, and it still
  steps down by itself if the graphics card cannot hold 60 FPS. If you never
  changed this setting from Native, it moves to Auto once; choose Native again
  in **Settings > Graphics** to keep the PS2 picture.
- Graphics cards: laptops with two graphics chips now run the game on the
  dedicated one. If the chosen internal resolution does not fit the card, the
  game uses the largest size that fits instead of falling back to Native.
- Performance: the game runs above normal priority and opts out of Windows
  power throttling.
- Every graphics setting was checked in game on the same frame: renderers,
  resolution, borderless, aspect ratios, FXAA, texture filtering, upscalers,
  internal resolution, HD textures and the frame rate cap.
- Music can still fail to load after a fight. `startup.log` now records when
  the music stream restarts and whether the sound chip ran out of music data;
  please attach it if this happens to you.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
