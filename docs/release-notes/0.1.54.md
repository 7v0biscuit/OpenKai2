# v0.1.54

Finding the rest of what keeps Budokai 3 bodies black and blue.

The flag set in v0.1.52 was not enough: forced Budokai 3 characters still
show black with a blue face. Supreme Kai's mesh is the same size in both
games, block for block, but every one of its 16 geometry blocks differs, and
the earlier reports only compared the first 512 bytes of one of them.

- With a Budokai 3 disc as the source, **Save disc report…** now also
  compares its Supreme Kai and Krillin meshes with your Budokai 2 disc's,
  word by word. Vertex positions, normals and texture coordinates are left
  out; every other difference (counts, flags and the codes that tell the
  console how to draw) is counted and the first 200 are listed with their
  rows. Choose your Budokai 2 ISO on the Play page first, then the Budokai 3
  ISO in the import tool, and send the report.

Nothing about imports changes in this version.

Not fixed yet: in Dragon World the character can walk off screen, and the
dialogue text and the Yes/No prompt after character select are invisible.

The original USA ISO is required.
