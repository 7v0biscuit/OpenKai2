# v0.2.9

- Use the Everyday layout with a six-tab dock: Play, Online, Speedrun, Mods,
  Settings and Updates. Controls and Saves & backups now open from Play, with
  a Back to Play button. Existing tab arrangements keep their relative order.
- Give the dock evenly sized tabs, centered icons, readable labels and a moving
  selection highlight. Keep patch notes on Play and the original theme artwork.
- Refine all thirteen theme palettes with clearer surface shades, cooler body
  text and distinct accents. Light themes use darker accent colors for readable
  links and focus states. Custom colors and selected artwork are preserved.
- Use a larger game title, a theme-colored Play button and two clear Play tools.
  Verify ISO replaces the Change action; its menu also offers another ISO.
- Find pages and tools from the header or Ctrl+K. Search, use the arrow keys and
  press Enter to open a result. Alt+1 to Alt+6 also follow the dock order.
- Search Settings by section, undo the last settings change, and reset Display
  or Game independently. Settings have more room for labels and dropdowns.
- Stack control bindings in narrower windows and widen controller fields so
  button names fit. Keyboard and controller assignments are preserved.
- Search saves and backup filenames, sort backups, and review a backup's contents
  before restoring. Recovery backups and multiple selection remain available.
- Respect a saved Discord presence preference instead of enabling it at startup
  or when saving unrelated options.

Includes v0.2.8's audio crackle fix, automatic internal resolution and graphics
card selection improvements. Music playback and mod tools remain in development.
