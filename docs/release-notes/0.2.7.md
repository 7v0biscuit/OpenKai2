# v0.2.7

- A new Match Details lounge on Play keeps friends, rooms and selected-room
  information together.
- Open a room, search by name or host, view its access and connection, and join
  from the details panel. Create rooms and find players through Online.
- Keep patch notes on Play and the full navigation dock at the bottom. Rounded
  panels and a moving dock highlight use the selected theme. Navigation motion
  follows Windows animation preferences and stops when the launcher is minimized.
- Give the existing hand-drawn artwork a larger area on Play, with controls placed
  away from the drawing and a fade behind the title. Quieter panel colors keep
  the illustration and theme accent in focus; light themes retain readable text.
- Anti-alias rounded panels, buttons, focus outlines, dropdown arrows, navigation
  icons, status dots and circular profile photos. Artwork follows the card’s
  smooth edge; straight separators stay aligned to physical pixels.
- Embed fixed regular and semibold font weights for consistent Win32 text, with
  a larger condensed game title and more space between the title and actions.
- Add your own PNG, JPEG or animated GIF as a profile picture. Choose a file
  from the Profile page or drop one onto it. Pictures appear on Play, Online
  and Profile, with circular cropping and an initials fallback.
- Keep pictures separate for each local profile. Removing a picture keeps
  its original file; saving initials, accents or taglines keeps the picture.
- Search rooms and players by name, clear a search in one click, or use Ctrl+F.
  Filter rooms by compatible version or password, and players by friends online.
- Keep friends in Online's Players list after adding them. Open profile
  customization directly from Online.
- Show Reconnect while offline and Refresh while connected. Keep list positions
  during presence and ping updates, and disable join/invite actions until ready.
- Keep Online's action buttons above the dock at standard window sizes, with
  scrolling for shorter windows. Joining a friend's locked room opens its
  password field, even when the room was hidden by a search or filter.

Profile pictures and appearance are saved on this PC and are not uploaded to
other players. The online service's match and identity protocols are unchanged.
