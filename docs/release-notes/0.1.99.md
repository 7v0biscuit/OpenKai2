# v0.1.99

A new launcher look with five picture themes, and Load state on the Play page.

- Launcher themes: **Capsule**, **Dragon Balls**, **Dragon Radar**, **Ki** and
  **Scouter** put a drawn picture in the Play banner and color the whole
  launcher to match it. Capsule is a light blueprint theme; the other four are
  dark. Pick one in Settings, under Theme.
- Easier on the eyes: the default theme now uses soft charcoal instead of
  near-black, with calmer glows. All text uses one clean typeface; the comic
  lettering is kept for the OpenKai2 logo only.
- Sidebar: tabs are grouped into **Game** and **Library**, with line icons.
  Online shows how many friends are online and Mods how many mods are on.
  Friends sit in their own box with green dots for friends who are online.
- Play page: **Load state** starts the game and loads save-state slot 1 as soon
  as the game is running, and the banner shows when that state was saved. The
  disc card shows when your disc is verified, with **Change…** and **Verify
  again**. A strip under the tiles shows what is new, with a link to the
  release notes.
- Fixed: changing the theme could leave the old background until the window
  was resized.
- Budokai 3 movesets (experimental): forcing a new character can now bring its
  Budokai 3 moves. The character animates, but buttons may not yet trigger the
  right moves.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
