# v0.5.2

Player profiles everyone can see, friend alerts, and a fullscreen launcher.

- **Profile pictures for everyone.** Your picture now reaches other players
  as a small signed copy, so they see it in the Players, Friends and Rooms
  lists instead of your initials. Your original file stays on your PC, and an
  animated GIF shares its first frame.
- **Name colors.** Pick an optional color for your name from eight presets or
  any custom color. Colors that would be hard to read are adjusted
  automatically.
- **More personality.** Add a main fighter, an About me, your region and your
  playstyle. Your online match count and the month you first went online are
  added for you. **Preview my card** shows your card exactly as other players
  see it, and you choose whether to share your picture and card, your card
  only, or nothing.
- **View other players' profiles.** Right-click any name in Players, Friends
  or Rooms to view their profile, invite them or join their room, add or
  remove a friend, copy their name or profile ID, or block them. The Profile
  button on Online and Ctrl+K search open profiles too; Ctrl+K also finds a
  player by a pasted profile ID.
- **Friends panel on Play.** Switch between all friends, online friends,
  everyone online and your recent opponents. Offline friends show when they
  were last seen, the panel shows how many friends are online, and the list
  has more room at the default window size.
- **Friend alerts.** A Windows notification tells you when a friend comes
  online. Turn it off per profile; Quiet mode, Do not disturb and a running
  game keep it silent.
- **Block players.** Blocked players' invites and friend requests are ignored
  and they are listed last.
- **Fullscreen launcher.** F11 now switches the launcher to borderless
  fullscreen and back to the same window. A maximized launcher no longer
  shrinks or breaks its layout when Windows display scaling changes or it
  moves to another monitor.
- **Shortcuts.** Ctrl+P opens your profile.
- **Smoother busy scenes.** Each draw costs the graphics thread less work,
  which helps scenes with many effects.

Both players need v0.5.2 to play together online and to see each other's
player cards.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
