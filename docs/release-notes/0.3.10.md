# v0.3.10

Smooth scrolling and a rollback readout.

- Launcher pages now glide when you turn the mouse wheel instead of jumping.
  The motion follows your display's refresh rate, up to 240 frames a second on
  a 240 Hz monitor, and moves at the same speed on any monitor. Turning the
  wheel quickly adds up to one continuous glide. Scrolling stays instant when
  Windows animations or the launcher's reduced motion setting are off.
- The in-game online bar shows **rollback N** while the game is correcting
  late inputs: the most frames it replayed in the last two seconds. It hides
  again once the connection is steady.

Both players need v0.3.10 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
