# v0.4.1

Motion smoothing for high refresh rate monitors.

- **Motion smoothing** (Settings > Display: Off, Smooth or Low latency) shows the
  game smoothly on 120, 144 and 240 Hz monitors. The game itself still runs at
  its normal 60 steps a second; OpenKai2 draws extra pictures between game
  frames with every character and object moved part of the way, one extra
  picture at 120 or 144 Hz and three at 240 Hz. **Smooth** blends between the
  last two frames and shows each frame about 17 ms later; **Low latency** adds
  no lag but projects motion forward, so a sudden stop can overshoot for a few
  milliseconds. It needs roughly two to four times the graphics card work, and
  turns itself off on 60 Hz monitors, with the CPU renderer, or if the card
  cannot keep up. It is off by default.

Both players need v0.4.1 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
