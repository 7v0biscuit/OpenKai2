# v0.5.8

A new Wave home interface for the launcher, notification pop-ups, profile
badges, and fixes for clipping and small glitches.

- **Wave home.** Turn it on in Settings > Launcher > Interface style. The launcher
  opens on an animated menu bar over a moving sky: Left and Right pick a
  category, Up and Down pick an item, Enter opens it. Esc on any main page,
  the Play tab or a click on the logo returns to the home.
- **Every page in the new style.** Settings, Online, Mods, Speedrun, Updates,
  Profile, Theme colors, Arrange tabs and the Speedrun overlay editor use glass
  panels and full-width rows with an info bar along the bottom. Back buttons
  return to the home.
- **Sky and emblem.** Choose a sky (Time of day, Saiyan sunset, Namek,
  Kaio-ken, Kami's lookout, Frieza night or Ki blue) and show or hide the
  four-star Dragon Ball behind the pages. The page colors follow the sky.
- **Notification pop-ups.** Friend requests, invitations and other alerts
  slide in at the top right and fade out. A count badge sits on the bell and
  on the Network category. Setting your status to Do not disturb keeps them
  quiet.
- **Profile badges and levels.** Fighter cards show badges earned from your
  matches and profile, such as Rookie, Z Warrior, Legend and Complete Card,
  plus a level bar showing matches to the next level.
- **Find.** Theme colors can now be found with Ctrl+K.
- **Mods folder path.** In smaller windows the folder path ran underneath the
  Install mod button. It now shortens with an ellipsis before the buttons.
- **D-pad arrows on Controls.** The arrow rows showed empty boxes on systems
  whose font has no arrow symbols. The arrows are now drawn as shapes.
- **Ctrl+A in text fields.** Ctrl+A selects all the text in any field.

The classic interface is still the default and is unchanged.

Both players need v0.5.8 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
