# v0.1.95

More themes and colors, and shareable launcher looks.

- Eight new themes: Ultra Instinct, Super Saiyan Blue, Kaioken, Majin, Shenron,
  Ginyu Force, and two light themes, Capsule Corp and Kami's Lookout.
- Ten new accent colors: Mint, Peach, Lavender, Turquoise, Rose, Olive, Navy,
  Crimson, Ice and Bronze.
- Copy colors and Paste colors (Colors page): copy your whole look, with the
  theme, accent and every changed color, as one line of text, and paste a look
  to apply it.
- Surprise me (Colors page): a random theme and accent.
- Open logs folder (Updates page): opens the folder with the launcher and game
  logs.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
