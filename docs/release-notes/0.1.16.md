# v0.1.16

Reduce vector scheduling and PATH1 graphics-transfer overhead.

- Copy known graphics payloads in bulk while the vector unit is waiting or
  draining a completed program. Stop at pending pipeline commits and keep
  instruction issues, tag parsing, final qword reads, and callbacks on their
  original guest cycles. Wrapped buffers retain their original byte order.
- Speed up full-lane dependency updates and partial-lane iteration. Retire
  register writes with SIMD sequence comparisons and masked bit-preserving
  copies. Preserve superseded writes, untouched lanes, and all 64-bit clocks
  and sequence numbers. Reuse fully initialized write slots without repeatedly
  clearing their payloads.
- Add 128 original-interpreter transfer snapshots covering packed, register-list,
  and image tags; chained tags; packets up to 64 KiB; wrapped buffers; callback
  memory changes; pending stores; and resumed execution. Retain the existing
  arithmetic, pipeline, rendering, and callback references. The vector suite
  contains 47 cases.

Three alternating Windows-binary measurements under Wine showed about 80%
lower cost in the 4 KiB graphics-transfer workload and 16% in its shorter
packet control. Eight multiply-add/subtract workloads cost 6–10% less. State,
cycle, memory, and packet hashes matched the previous runtime. These are
synthetic execution costs and do not establish game frame rates.

The original USA ISO remains required at launch. Host audio remains disabled.
Complete matches and sustained 60 FPS remain unverified.
