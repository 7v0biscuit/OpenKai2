# v0.1.90

Steadier picture at higher internal resolution, more anti-aliasing, HD texture
comparison and a more personal speedrun overlay.

- Internal resolution (2x, 3x, 4x): the picture no longer shakes. The game
  sends alternating odd and even lines; at higher resolutions odd frames were
  drawn one line low, so the picture bounced. Both now line up as at native
  resolution.
- Full-size frames reach the window without extra copies, and 4-bit texture
  uploads (snow and other particle stages) are written a row at a time.
- Anti-aliasing has two new levels: FXAA High and FXAA Ultra. They follow
  edges further than FXAA, so long edges are smoother.
- In game, F4 switches HD textures off and on to compare them with the
  originals, and shows how many textures were replaced.
- Settings: the Game section is above Graphics, and the FPS counter moved
  there from the Speedrun page.
- Speedrun overlay: add your own title and split names, choose the distance
  from the screen edge, rounded or square corners, an Extra large size, and
  Faint or Solid backgrounds.

At 4x the graphics card does sixteen times the work of native resolution; on
busy stages such as the snow stage, 2x or 3x keeps a steadier 60 FPS.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
