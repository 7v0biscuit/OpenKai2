# v0.1.102

Nine new launcher themes in the hand-printed style.

- New themes: **Budokai Black** ("武道会" in brush lettering over a red ink
  sun, on an all-black launcher), **Budokai Black (board)** (a hand-drawn
  Dragon World board, all black with a red Play button), **Majin Buu** (a
  candy beam turning things into candy), **Babidi** (a crystal ball swirling
  with magic), **Majin Vegeta** (a dark golden aura crackling with
  lightning), **Saiyan Pod**, **Spirit Bomb**, **Flying Nimbus** and
  **Potara**.
- The Ki theme is gone. If you used it, the launcher moves you to Majin
  Vegeta; every other theme stays as you had it.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
