# v0.6.2

A new profile page, more ways to style your card, skill achievements and
smoother launcher motion.

- **New profile page.** On the Wave home, the Profile page opens with your card
  as players see it, with your picture controls beside it. **Card look**,
  **About you**, **Privacy & alerts** and the save buttons sit below.
- **More card options.** Sixteen frames, sixteen card designs (new: Kame House
  sea, Time chamber void, Red planet, Lookout clouds, Lava wasteland, Snowy
  peaks, Cell Games ring and Aurora) and thirty-seven titles. **Frame color**
  tints any frame, and **Featured achievement** shows one of your achievements
  under your name. New badges cover speedruns, 500 wins, win streaks and Z6
  wins. Launchers older than v0.6.2 can't show cards from v0.6.2 or later.
- **Thirteen skill achievements, 42 in all.** Beat a CPU on Z4 or Z6, beat Z6
  without losing health or from under a tenth of your health, beat Z4 or higher
  in under 20 seconds, win three flawless battles in a row, win without dropping
  below three quarters of your health, turn around a fight from under a quarter
  of your health, win in under 6 seconds, win a time-out by less than 5%, win
  flawless online or three online battles in a row, and finish a speedrun
  without losing a battle. Z level achievements use the lowest level of the
  fight and only count against the CPU.
- **More colors.** Twelve new name colors and eight new accent colors.
- **More launcher options.** Four new Wave skies (Time chamber, Planet Vegeta,
  Kame House dusk and Majin pink), a **Clock** setting (Windows format, 12-hour,
  24-hour or hidden) and **Wave ribbons** (Lively, Calm or Off).
- **Smoother motion.** Animations are timed with a high-resolution clock, and
  on Wave pages buttons and lists appear once the page has faded in instead of
  popping in first.
- **Fixes.** The glow behind Wave category icons and page icons no longer leaves
  a dark square inside the icon, the Settings icon's knobs are no longer dark
  dots, and the page breadcrumb shows the full path from the first frame.
