# v0.5.9

Nicer profile colors, profile themes for tagged players, and launcher fixes.

- **Smoother name effects.** Every name effect except the [DEV] Super Saiyan
  effect has new colors that flow smoothly from letter to letter, with no hard
  flashes. Effects animate at up to 60 frames per second.
- **Tag colors.** The [TTV], [YT] and [ToB] labels shimmer in their tag colors.
  The [DEV] label keeps its color.
- **Profile themes.** Verified [DEV], [TTV], [YT] and [ToB] players get a
  tinted player card with a glowing edge and the theme name. A verified tag is
  now recognized by everyone, even without a name effect.
- **Name colors.** The presets have refreshed tones that shade softly across
  the name, and four new ones: Vegeta blue, Senzu green, Android teal and Ultra
  Instinct silver. Super Saiyan gold is unchanged. Colors picked earlier switch
  to the new tones automatically.
- **Colored names in online matches.** The in-game online banner shows your
  opponent's name with their name color, tag colors and name effect.
- **Profiles move to the Profile page.** Adding, renaming, removing and
  switching profiles is now on the Profile page. The Online page keeps
  Customize profile and Copy profile ID, which saves space.
- **Launcher fixes.** Empty Players and Rooms lists show proper icons instead of
  stray letters. All Wave home categories stay visible on wide windows. The
  address box no longer touches the Join address button. Profile text fields
  line up with the dropdowns. The Wave home animates at full frame rate while
  the launcher is in front, and controls are repainted cleanly after scrolling.

Both players need v0.5.9 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
