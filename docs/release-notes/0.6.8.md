# v0.6.8

Better launcher alerts, a moving background on every page, smoother 4x frame
rates and Budokai 3 characters that fight.

- **Smoother frames at 2x to 4x.** Every new frame at a higher internal
  resolution was handed to the window in a fresh buffer, about 15 MB at 4x,
  and copied twice more on the way. Frames are now copied out of the graphics
  card once into a buffer reused from frame to frame, and the window uploads
  it without another copy. That is about 3.5 ms less work per frame at 4x,
  where heavy fight scenes needed up to 19 ms per frame and dropped to about
  48 FPS. The drawing shaders also tell the graphics driver which data they
  only read, so it can cache it.
- **New alerts.** Alerts stack at the top right, up to three at a time, each
  with its own icon and colour. They slide in and out, show a countdown bar,
  pause while you point at them, close with their x and open Notifications
  when clicked, and wait while the launcher is minimized. Every friend alert
  in a batch is shown, not only the last.
- **More alerts.** A summary when the game closes, trophies unlocked during
  the session, a found update and mod imports that finish or fail. The
  Notifications page lists this session's alerts under Recent with Clear all,
  and the bell shows a dot for alerts you have not seen.
- **Moving background everywhere.** The galaxy scene now moves behind every
  page and shows faintly through the glass panels.
- **Smoother launcher.** Buttons and section chips fade on hover, pages glide
  further per wheel step with a softer ease and stop at either end, and a slim
  scroll bar shows while you scroll.
- **Play time and resume.** Play shows when you last played and your total
  play time, and the home menu opens where you left it.
- **Budokai 3 characters fight.** Imported Budokai 3 characters now punch,
  kick and chain their hits in their Budokai 3 animations, buttons play the
  Budokai 3 move they belong to, and each character gets its own effects
  instead of its neighbour's. 171 of Budokai 3's 175 models now convert,
  including most of Goku's and Vegeta's forms. Ki blasts still show their
  stance and fire nothing.
