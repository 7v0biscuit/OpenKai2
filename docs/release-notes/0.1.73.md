# v0.1.73

Online joining that works without setup, a cleaner launcher and smoother pacing.

## Online

- Fixed rooms without a password turning away every player.
- When a player can't reach a room directly, both launchers now connect
  through the online service and the match runs through it automatically. No
  port forwarding is needed. A direct connection is still tried first.
- Rooms are listed even when the host's public address can't be found, so
  invites to them work.
- The room list shows "via relay" instead of "no reply".
- `launcher.log` records each step of joining or hosting, and why it failed.

## Launcher

- Fixed white boxes and leftover controls flashing when switching tabs.

## Performance

- Frame deadlines are kept precisely on Windows. Before, frames could start up
  to about 16 ms late, which made the pace uneven even in menus.
- The GPU renderer sends much larger batches, which lightens stages with many
  particles.

The original USA ISO is required.
