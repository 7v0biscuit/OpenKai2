# v0.3.8

Smoother launcher scrolling and time sync for online matches.

- The launcher now draws each scrolled frame off screen and shows it whole, so
  controls no longer flicker, vanish or appear twice while the page scrolls.
- Buttons and dropdowns that scroll out from under the pointer no longer keep
  their highlight.
- Online matches keep both games in step. When one PC runs slightly faster and
  drifts more than a frame ahead, it waits a frame or two so it stops running
  out of the other player's inputs. In testing with one player 5% fast, waits
  for late inputs dropped from 79 to 1.

Both players need v0.3.8 to play together.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
