# v0.1.78

A cleaner Splits panel on the Speedrun page.

## Speedrun mode

- Splits are shown in columns: split, time, segment and best segment. Segments
  that match your best segment are shown in gold.
- A summary line shows the best run, sum of best and possible time save, and
  the panel title shows attempts and finished runs.
- The panel shows which split is selected.
- The rules panel shows the first rules and an **All rules…** button opens the
  full list. The permadeath rule now reads: being knocked out in a Dragon World
  fight, or losing one on time, ends the run.

The original USA ISO is required.
