# v0.1.67

A rebuilt online page with live rooms, friends and invites, a reserved [DEV]
tag, and a new launcher layout.

## Online

- The launcher connects to an online service by itself. Rooms and players
  update live; there is nothing to refresh.
- Rooms show their ping. Rooms from another OpenKai2 version are marked.
- **Friends:** pick a player and press **Add friend**. A request sent while
  they are offline arrives when they come back. Friends are listed first, with
  their status.
- **Invites:** pick a player and press **Invite**. Your room opens and they get
  a Windows notification and an **Accept** / **Decline** banner. Invited
  players join without the room password.
- Rooms can be **Public**, **Invite only** (hidden from the list) or **Not
  listed**.
- The host's router port is opened automatically when the router allows it
  (UPnP).
- **Auto input delay** (the new default) picks the delay from the ping when a
  player joins.
- Each profile has its own key. Presence, invites and friend requests are
  signed with it, so they can't be faked.
- The **[DEV]** tag is reserved for the developer; other names lose it.
- The service is a public MQTT broker (broker.emqx.io). Match traffic still goes
  directly between the two players. `OpenKai2Lobby` can be run as your own
  service.
- The PS2 clock and memory card dates are now the same on both players' games,
  which fixes a desync.

## Launcher

- The sidebar is now **Play, Online, Speedrun, Mods, Saves, Settings**
  (Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+6).
- **Speedrun** has its own page with the category, permadeath, FPS counter,
  overlay settings and a summary of the timing rules.
- Settings ends with **Updates**.

## Other

- The game logs GPU waits by reason every 30 seconds
  (`[renderer:wait:sample]`), to find what slows down fights.

The original USA ISO is required.
