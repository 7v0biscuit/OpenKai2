# v0.2.2

Rebind your controls, and a cleaner launcher.

- Controls: a new **Controls** tab lists every PS2 button with a key, an
  alternate key and a controller button. Click a key box and press the new key,
  right-click to clear it, and pick controller buttons from a list. A key moves
  to the button you give it, and **Reset to defaults** restores the usual
  controls. The game uses your bindings from its next start, for the keyboard
  and every controller. F1 to F12 and Ctrl stay reserved for OpenKai2 shortcuts.
- Play page: the game's artwork now fills the top of the page, with the game's
  status and a larger Play button that glows when everything is ready. The
  keyboard row moved to the Controls tab.
- Sidebar: tabs are grouped into Game, Library and System, and your profile's
  avatar, name and online state sit at the bottom.
- Every page: matching panel headings, a status pill in the header, and a status
  bar showing the connection and version.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
