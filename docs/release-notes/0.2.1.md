# v0.2.1

A clearer Online page, with better notifications, profiles and friends list.

- Online page: your profile card shows your avatar, whether you are online,
  and how many friends are online. Hosting a room takes one row, so the room
  and player lists have more space. The password box only appears when the
  room you pick needs one.
- Rooms and players: each row shows the host's or player's initials, the room
  name, the ping, and tags for passwords, other versions and unverified names.
  Rooms on your version with the lowest ping come first.
- Friends: friends who are online come first, with a dot for online, in a
  match or offline. A friend who is hosting shows the room's name, and
  double-clicking them joins it. Double-clicking any other friend invites them.
- Notifications: invites and friend requests show who sent them, when, which
  room, and how many more are waiting. Repeat invites from the same player are
  merged, and the Online tab shows how many are waiting.
- Profiles: checking a new profile name no longer freezes the launcher while
  it waits for the online service.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
