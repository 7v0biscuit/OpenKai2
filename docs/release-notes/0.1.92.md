# v0.1.92

Make the launcher your own, a dedicated Updates page and steadier updates.

- Themes: Ember (the original look), Ocean, Forest, Violet, Crimson and
  Graphite. Each colors the background, panels, sidebar and buttons, and
  changes apply at once.
- Accent colors: keep the theme's own color, or choose Orange, Gold, Red,
  Pink, Purple, Blue, Cyan, Green or White for buttons and highlights.
- Arrange tabs: move tabs up or down and hide the ones you do not use (Settings,
  Launcher, Arrange tabs…). Play and Settings are always shown, and Ctrl+1 to
  Ctrl+7 follow your order.
- Start on: open the launcher on Play or on the last page you used.
- Updates has its own page in the sidebar with your version, what is
  available, the update channel, automatic updates, Check now and Install.
- Updates retry when GitHub does not answer, the connection drops or a
  download is interrupted, instead of stopping after the first error.

If an update from v0.1.90 or earlier stops with "HTTP 502", "503" or "504",
download this version from the release page once; later updates retry on
their own.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
