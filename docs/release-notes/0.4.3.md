# v0.4.3

Graphics options that work, and more of them.

- **Internal resolution now stays where you put it.** Choosing Native, 2x, 3x or
  4x keeps that resolution for the whole session. Before, the game could quietly
  drop back to Native whenever frames ran late. Only **Auto** adjusts itself.
- **AMD FSR 1 matters at high resolutions too.** When the picture is larger than
  the window, FSR's sharpening now runs after it is scaled down.
- **New anti-aliasing:** NFAA (smooths outlines without softening flat areas or
  text) and DLAA (strongest on long, straight edges like the horizon).
- **New upscalers:** Mitchell (a softer bicubic with less ringing) and Integer
  scaling (every pixel the same size; needs a 1080p screen for 2x).
- **New Settings > Graphics > Image effect:** Sharpen, Vibrant colors, CRT
  scanlines, Sharpen + vibrant and Soft glow.
- **New Settings > Graphics > Brightness:** Darker, Normal, Brighter and Much
  brighter.
- The Play page title now reads **Play**, and the settings filter buttons no
  longer leave "All settings" highlighted after choosing another section.

Both players need v0.4.3 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
