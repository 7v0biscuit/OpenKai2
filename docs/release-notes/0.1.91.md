# v0.1.91

Sharper internal resolution, smoother audio and more reliable updates.

- Internal resolution (2x, 3x, 4x): when the game draws a picture and then uses
  it as a texture (full-screen copies and effects), that picture now keeps the
  higher resolution. Before, it was read back at native resolution and
  stretched, which made the screen look pixelated and hid the effect of
  anti-aliasing.
- Audio: when the audio buffer runs too full, playback now catches up with a
  short crossfade instead of jumping, which caused a pop. Small speed
  corrections use smoother interpolation, so they no longer dull the sound.
- Updates: if GitHub's download servers do not respond, which can happen in
  the first minutes after a release, the launcher now retries a few times
  instead of stopping with "HTTP 504".

At 4x the graphics card draws sixteen times the pixels of native resolution;
on busy stages such as the snow stage, 2x or 3x keeps 60 FPS.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
