# v0.1.94

Randomizer mode and a cleaner Colors page.

- Randomizer (Settings, Game, and the Speedrun page): every fight in Dragon
  World and the World Tournament gives you a random character from the
  Budokai 2 roster, with that character's own moves and skills. Every fight and
  every retry after a loss picks again, and the same character never comes
  twice in a row. A message names your new character when the fight starts.
- Only your character changes. The CPU's character, skills and difficulty stay
  as the game set them. Dueling and Babidi's Spaceship are not affected, and
  the randomizer is off in online play.
- Speedruns: the randomizer works with every category and with permadeath.
  Randomizer runs are their own categories (for example "Any% Randomizer") with
  their own splits and run logs.
- Colors page: each part shows a larger swatch, its name and what it colors,
  and parts you changed are marked; color codes and repeated notes are gone.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
