# v0.6

Achievements, the Wave home as the default launcher style, and launcher polish.

- **Achievements.** Fourteen built-in OpenKai2 achievements are tracked while you
  play, from your first win to World Champion. Unlocks slide in at the top of the
  screen. Open **Achievements** from the Wave home's Game menu or with Ctrl+K.
- **RetroAchievements.** Sign in with your free retroachievements.org account on
  the Achievements page. OpenKai2 keeps only the sign-in token, watches the game
  and sends unlocks to your account. **Hardcore** turns off save states, mods,
  CPU difficulty and the randomizer while you play. The supported USA disc is not
  linked to the RetroAchievements Budokai 2 set yet; the page says so and shows
  the disc hash to request it.
- **Unlock all pauses achievements.** So it can't earn them for you, Unlock all
  pauses built-in achievements and RetroAchievements until you restore a backup
  made before it, or delete the Budokai 2 save.
- **Wave home is the default.** The animated menu bar is now the launcher's
  default style. Settings from earlier versions switch to it once; choose
  Classic in Settings > Launcher to keep the dock.
- **Animations.** Page titles slide in, panels fade up with a light sweep along
  their edges, and the page icon glows as you open a page.
- **No leftovers when going back.** Backing out of a page to the home screen no
  longer leaves pieces of the previous page behind, including with animated
  names.
- **Settings that work in Wave home.** Start on, Arrange tabs, custom panel,
  field, border and text colors, and the Dragon Ball colors now apply in Wave
  home. Theme color parts that only exist in Classic are hidden there.
- **More colors.** Eight new themes (Super Saiyan Blue, Ultra Instinct, Namek
  Sky, Frieza Frost, Kaio-ken Crimson, Planet Vegeta Dusk, Capsule Corp Navy and
  Time Chamber) and five new accents (Super Saiyan Blue, Ultra Instinct silver,
  Super Saiyan Rose, Hakai purple and Senzu lime).

Both players need v0.6 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
