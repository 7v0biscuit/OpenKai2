# v0.6.3

Smoother launcher motion, redrawn profile frames and Dragon Ball Z card designs.

- **Smoother pages.** Opening a page no longer repaints the whole page on every
  animation frame, page fades blend faster, and buttons and lists on Wave pages
  stay hidden until the page has faded in, so nothing flashes or is left behind.
- **Clock fits.** The date and time at the top right are no longer cut off,
  including with the 12-hour clock.
- **Redesigned player card.** Your card on the Profile page is now a
  Budokai 2 style panel: thick ink border with corner brackets, your name on a
  slanted accent banner with outlined lettering, a title ribbon, a medal for
  your featured achievement, a main fighter plate, an aura burst behind your
  picture, a gold level badge, a segmented experience bar and a stats strip.
- **Redrawn frames.** Every picture frame is redrawn in a flat cel-shaded comic
  style with thick ink outlines and hard shadows: Saiyan aura flames, Kamehameha
  swooshes, a Shenron coil with horns and ridges, a Scouter lens, Frieza armor
  plates, a Destructo disc saw, metal rivets and more.
- **Dragon Ball Z card designs.** Card designs are redrawn as comic panels with
  halftone dots and ink lines, and each one is a Dragon Ball Z place or effect:
  Saiyan space pod, battle speed lines, Namek, the World Tournament, Capsule
  Corp, the Dragon Radar, Kame House, the Hyperbolic Time Chamber, Planet
  Vegeta, Kami's Lookout, Dying Namek, a rocky wasteland, the Cell Games arena
  and King Kai's planet.
- **Developer titles.** Profiles with the developer key get four developer-only
  titles and can use every title and feature any achievement. Other players
  only see developer titles on a verified developer card.
