# v0.5.7

Dragon Ball Z name effects with moving letters, live previews, channel
shortcuts and a steadier profile ring. The [DEV] key can now use every effect.

- **Everything is Dragon Ball Z.** All name effects, name colors and accent
  colors now carry Dragon Ball Z names, such as Super Saiyan, Galick Gun,
  Kaio-ken, Kamehameha, Frieza purple and Piccolo green.
- **Four new moving effects.** Powering up ([DEV]), Ginyu Force ([TTV]),
  Dynamite Kick ([YT]) and Kid Buu rampage ([ToB]) move the letters of the
  name as well as their colors.
- **[DEV] gets every effect.** The developer key can use all 30 effects,
  including those of the other tags.
- **Live previews.** The **Name color** list shows every preset in its color and
  every effect in its own colors.
- **Channel shortcuts.** Right-click a player with a channel link for
  **Open channel** and **Copy channel link**.
- **Steadier profile ring.** The animated ring around tagged avatars blends
  smoothly instead of jumping between colors.

Older launchers show the new effects, and [DEV] profiles using another tag's
effect, as a plain name. Both players need v0.5.7 to play together online.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
