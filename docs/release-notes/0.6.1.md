# v0.6.1

Profile card styles, speedrun achievements, gentler Z4-Z6 recovery and a cleaner
Wave home.

- **Card style.** On the Profile page, pick one of twelve picture frames, eight
  card backdrops and sixteen titles. Some titles need a level and four need an
  achievement (World Champion, Saga Hero, Untouchable and Hercule's Rival).
  Your frame, backdrop and title show on your player card online. Launchers
  older than v0.6.1 can't read these cards.
- **More badges.** New badges for achievements and play, wrapping onto extra
  rows when there are many.
- **Fifteen new achievements, 29 in all.** Eight are for finished speedruns:
  any run, a World Tournament run, an Any%, New Game+ or All Dragon Balls run,
  Hercule Only, permadeath, the CPU on Z4 or higher, a verified run with nothing
  flagged and a new personal best. Also new: 500 wins, 100 battles, 5 wins in a
  row, a win in under 10 seconds, a win on under 2% health, 10 online wins and
  50 battles in one session.
- **Z4-Z6 recover more slowly.** CPU health recovery is now about 0.12%, 0.24%
  and 0.36% a second. It starts only after the CPU goes three seconds without
  being hit, stops when it is hit and never heals past three quarters.
- **Wave pages reworked.** Each page shows where you are (for example Home ›
  Settings › Display) and Esc goes back. Buttons that repeated a Wave item and
  the Back buttons are gone, Find a player (which reopened Online) is removed,
  Settings shows only the section you chose, and panels take their tint from
  the sky.
- **Smoother page changes.** Opening a page or going back slides and fades
  between them, in the direction you are going.
- **Cleaner icons and text.** Wave menu icons, the notification bell and locked
  trophies no longer sit in boxes, and captions use the Inter font.
- **Fixes.** The dark seam across the middle of the Wave sky is gone, opening a
  Wave item no longer flashes the Play page, Release notes opens in your browser
  without leaving the menu, and long status messages no longer run into the key
  hints.
