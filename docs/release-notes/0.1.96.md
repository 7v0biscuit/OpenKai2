# v0.1.96

The randomizer now swaps your whole character in Dragon World, and the
launcher themes were reworked.

- Randomizer: in Dragon World the random character now appears as its own full
  model, with its head, portrait, moves and skills, instead of a headless copy
  of the ally you picked. The story scene and the "Battle!" screen still show
  the ally you chose; the fight then loads the random character. World
  Tournament fights are unchanged.
- Themes: eleven themes instead of twenty, with the near-duplicates removed:
  Ember, Super Saiyan Blue, Namek, Frieza, Kaioken, Majin, Saiyan Gold,
  Graphite, Midnight (true black), Daylight and Capsule Corp. Each has its own
  background, panels, Play banner and text colors. A theme from an older
  version moves to the closest one, and copied color codes from older versions
  still paste.
- Themes now reach the whole launcher: the title bar, its text and border,
  scrollbars and dropdown lists follow the theme, and light themes get a light
  title bar.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
