# v0.6.4

Space card designs, a starry launcher sky and profile card fixes.

- **Card designs are now space scenes.** Midnight sky, Andromeda, Rose nebula,
  Emerald nebula, Golden galaxy, Crimson void, Frozen cosmos, Violet storm,
  Milky Way, Twin suns, Cosmic dawn, Supernova, Ringed planet, Starfall and
  Black hole, each with its own starfield and colors.
- **Starry launcher sky.** Small stars twinkle slowly behind every Wave page and
  a shooting star crosses every few seconds. They only appear where the
  background shows, never over panels or text. A new **Starry night** Wave sky
  matches them. With reduced motion the stars stay still.
- **Smoother animation.** Animations run on a 16 ms timer with 1 ms timer
  precision while they play, for steadier 60 FPS motion.
- **Card look saves.** **Save profile** now keeps your frame, frame color, card
  design, title and featured achievement, including for developer key
  profiles. Before, they were only kept when you changed your picture.
- **Pages show their controls on time.** Buttons and lists on Wave pages appear
  as soon as the page fades in, even with an animated profile picture. Before,
  they could take a few seconds.
- **Animated pictures cost less.** An animated GIF profile picture now redraws
  only itself instead of the whole page on every frame.
- **Profile card fixes.** The level badge no longer flashes, frames are no
  longer cut off or drawn over themselves with animated accents or name
  effects, and text next to an animated picture no longer flickers.
