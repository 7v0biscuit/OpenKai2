# v0.1.101

Smoother music and steadier frame rates.

- Music: when the game ran slightly below full speed (around 57 to 59 FPS),
  music switched to time stretching and parts of it played a second time, with
  pops at the joins. Small slowdowns are now absorbed by nudging the playback
  rate, stretching is kept for a game that is clearly running slow (below
  about 94% speed), and after a hitch the music fades briefly and carries on
  from where it was instead of starting over.
- Frame rate: the internal resolution you choose is now a maximum. When the
  graphics card cannot keep 60 FPS, the game lowers the internal resolution a
  step at a time, and raises it again once there is headroom. Each change is
  written to `startup.log`. Slowdowns caused by the CPU are not affected.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
