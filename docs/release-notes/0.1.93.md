# v0.1.93

Recolor every part of the launcher.

- Colors…: Settings, Launcher, Colors… lists every color the launcher uses,
  with a swatch and its value: background, sidebar, panels, fields, buttons
  and their hover color, borders, the three text shades, accent, highlight,
  glow, main buttons and their text, both sides of the Play banner with its
  title and details, success, error and link colors, and the Dragon Ball with
  its stars. Choose Change color… (or double-click a row) to pick any color
  with the Windows color picker. Use theme color undoes one part and Reset all
  colors undoes them all. Changes apply at once.
- Six more themes: Saiyan Gold, Namek, Frieza, Midnight, Sakura and Daylight,
  a light theme with dark text.
- Eight more accent colors: Teal, Lime, Magenta, Coral, Sky, Indigo, Silver and
  Amber.

Your existing theme, accent and tab order are kept.

Download **OpenKai2.exe** for the portable app, **OpenKai2-windows-x64.zip** to
extract manually, or **OpenKai2-Setup.exe** to install. The original Budokai 2
USA ISO is required at every launch. Checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
