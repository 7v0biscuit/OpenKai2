# Achievements

OpenKai2 has two kinds of achievements. Open them from **Achievements** on the
Wave home's Game menu, or search for "achievements" with Ctrl+K.

## Built-in achievements

Fourteen OpenKai2 achievements are tracked in the game on your PC. You don't
need an account for them. They include winning battles (1, 10, 50 and 200 wins),
a flawless win, a comeback with under a tenth of your health, a time-out win,
a win in under 20 seconds, a rematch win after a loss, starting Dragon World,
reaching the credits, winning the World Tournament, an online win and fighting
20 battles in one session.

An unlock slides in at the top of the screen with its points. Progress is kept
in `achievements.ini` next to the launcher settings.

## RetroAchievements

Sign in on the Achievements page with your free
[retroachievements.org](https://retroachievements.org/) account. The launcher
sends your password once to RetroAchievements and keeps only the sign-in token
it returns. **Sign out** forgets the token and the cached set.

When you play, OpenKai2 identifies the disc the same way RetroAchievements
emulators do, loads the Dragon Ball Z: Budokai 2 set, watches game memory each
frame and sends unlocks to your account. Unlocks also slide in at the top of the
screen. If RetroAchievements can't be reached, unlocks are kept and sent when it
is back.

- **Softcore** (the default) allows save states and mods.
- **Hardcore** turns off save states, mods, CPU difficulty changes and the
  randomizer while you play, as RetroAchievements' hardcore rules require.

The disc OpenKai2 supports (Budokai 2 USA, SLUS-207.79) currently has the hash
`9358fc389ad2873c0a15653de9b1ce68`, which RetroAchievements has not linked to
its Budokai 2 set yet. Until it is linked, the Achievements page and the game
say so and the built-in achievements keep working. **Copy disc hash** and
**Open set page** help you ask for the hash to be linked.

## Unlock all

**Unlock all** on the Saves page unlocks every character, stage and capsule on
your memory card. So that it cannot earn achievements for you, it pauses both
built-in achievements and RetroAchievements. The Achievements page shows the
pause. Achievements resume when you restore a backup made before Unlock all, or
delete the Budokai 2 save.
